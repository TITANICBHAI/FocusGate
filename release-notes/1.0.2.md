---
tagline: A new look, a Firefox version, and one password that protects your rules.
---
- **A whole new look.** The side panel, dashboard, block page and setup are all redone, in light and dark.
- **Now on Firefox.** A Firefox build ships next to Chrome and Edge.
- **One password protects your rules.** Type your own or generate one. Removing or loosening a rule asks for it.
- **Schedules that explain themselves.** See what is on now, what starts next and your whole week. Start from a Work hours, Study or Sleep template.
- **A panel that says what it is doing.** A status strip tells you what is blocking, until when, and offers the next step.
- **An honest daily goal.** Only finished sessions count toward your goal, streak and achievements.
- **A calm finish.** A done card, a soft chime you can turn off, and a moment when you reach your goal.
- **Your reason, right on the block page.** See why you are focusing at the moment you need it.
- **Changes apply instantly.** Edits to sites, keywords, schedules and limits reach the blocker straight away, and Alt+Shift+B blocks the current site at once.
- **Easier for everyone.** Better screen-reader support, clear focus rings, a Keyboard shortcuts card, and a panel that fits 200% zoom.
