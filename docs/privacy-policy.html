Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

At FocusGate, we believe your data is yours. This Privacy Policy explains how the FocusGate browser extension stores and uses information. FocusGate is designed so the extension does not collect or transmit personal information. It applies to the extension in Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox, and to this website.

1. Data storage is 100% local

FocusGate is designed to operate entirely on your device. Your settings, blocked websites, keywords, focus history, schedules, daily limits, habits, notes and statistics are stored locally using your browser's extension storage on your computer. FocusGate keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, never the password itself.

We do not operate a server, database, or cloud storage for extension data. Your FocusGate data stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself, such as when you export a backup or open a support issue.

2. No analytics or tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics services (like Google Analytics or Mixpanel) inside the extension. We do not track how you use the extension, which websites you block, or how often you fail a focus session. The Firefox version declares that it collects no data.

3. Permissions explained

To function correctly, FocusGate requires certain permissions in your browser. Here is exactly why we need them:

  • storage: Used to save your settings, blocked lists, and statistics locally on your device.
  • tabs & webNavigation: Used to detect when you attempt to visit a blocked website and intervene when a page or route changes, and to know which tab is in use for daily limits.
  • All-sites host access (<all_urls>): Used to inspect and redirect pages across websites selected by you. Page contents and browsing data are not uploaded.
  • declarativeNetRequest: Used to efficiently block web requests to distracting sites at the browser network level.
  • alarms: Used to run background timers for focus sessions, daily allowances, scheduled blocks, and weekly digests.
  • notifications: Used to send you alerts, such as when a focus session ends or when your weekly digest is ready.
  • idle: Used to stop counting time toward daily limits when you step away from the computer.
  • sidePanel (Chrome and Edge): Used to show the FocusGate timer and rules in the browser's side panel. Firefox uses its sidebar instead, which needs no extra permission.

FocusGate also runs a small script on YouTube, Google, Reddit, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn pages to apply keyword and content filters. That script works on your device and does not send page contents anywhere.

4. This website

This website is a static site. It has no forms, no accounts, no cookies of its own and no analytics. The service that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs under its own policy.

5. Third-party links

Our extension and website contain links to third-party websites, such as GitHub, Firefox Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

6. Changes to this policy

Since we do not collect data, this policy is unlikely to change drastically. However, we may update it from time to time to reflect changes in the extension's features. Any updates will be reflected on this page, with the date above.

7. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the open-source code, visit the Support page, inspect the source code on GitHub, or open an issue in our repository.
